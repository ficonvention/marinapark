Marina Park

About us

Three spaces at Seoraenaru on the Han River, Banpo

1F

Marina Cafe

The ground floor of Marina Park is a cafe overlooking the Han River. Enjoy coffee, tea, desserts, pizza, pasta and wine in an exotic setting; the cafe garden hosts birthday gatherings, club meetings and small parties. Open daily 10:00 – 22:00.

Cafe Menu Cafe Website

2F

Convention Hall

The 2nd-floor Convention Hall hosts year-end parties, corporate banquets, launch shows, MICE group events, family celebrations and weddings, with dining setups for 100 to 300 guests. It is fully equipped with a stage, LED screen and sound system, and floor-to-ceiling glass on all sides brings the Han River right into the room.

Business Party

3F

Rooftop Garden

The 3rd-floor Rooftop Garden, with a white marble gazebo on a marble platform, looks straight down onto the Han River and blends with the greenery of Seoripul Park. It is used for garden ceremonies, cocktail receptions, sunset parties and after-parties; in case of rain, events move to the 2F hall.
