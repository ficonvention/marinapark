FAQ

Marina Cafe FAQ

What are the cafe's opening hours?

Open daily 10:00 – 22:00. Some areas may be limited on days with private events.

Can we hold a small gathering or birthday party at the cafe?

The 1F cafe garden takes bookings for birthday gatherings, club meetings, bridal showers and small year-end parties of 10–30 people. You may bring a cake and simple decorations.

Where do we park?

Use the Banpo Han River Park lot. A perfect stop after a walk along the river.

Private parties at the cafe garden

Host a small party or gathering of 10–30 in the 1F cafe garden. · Daily 10:00 – 22:00
