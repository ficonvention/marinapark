FAQ

Intimate wedding FAQ

What is the minimum guest count for an intimate wedding?

A guaranteed minimum of 50 guests. The venue is ideal for intimate weddings of 50–100 family and friends; indoor hall ceremonies take 100 to 300, and garden ceremonies on the 3rd floor continue to a reception in the 2F hall.

How many weddings are held per day?

Only two couples a day, with generous time between ceremonies for preparation, photos and the reception.

Can we hold a ceremony without an officiant, or a family-dinner style wedding?

Yes. We design the flow to suit your style — ceremonies without an officiant, small weddings centred on a family dinner, or outdoor vows followed by a hall reception.

What happens if it rains?

If it rains on a garden-ceremony day, the ceremony moves indoors to the 2F Convention Hall. The rain setup is confirmed at your site visit, so there is no confusion on the day.

Is there a bridal room and a pyebaek room?

There is a bridal room with a river view, and we prepare a space for pyebaek and family photos.

Can we bring our own florist, photographer or make-up artist?

Yes. Floral décor is prepared through a concept meeting with our florist, and outside vendors are welcome by prior arrangement.

How is the menu composed?

Choose a chef-prepared course dinner (from ₩100,000 per person) or a buffet; menus are adjusted for your guests (seniors, children, international guests). Halal and vegetarian menus are available.

What are the ceremony times and how early should we book?

Weekend ceremonies run at 12:00 and 18:00, two couples a day. Indoor ceremonies are available year-round; garden ceremonies from late March to June and September to November. For spring and autumn weekends we recommend booking six months or more ahead.
