Product launches, brand promotions and media days for 100–300 guests: stage, LED screen, sound and lighting in a glass-walled river-view hall, plus a rooftop garden for photo moments and after-parties.

FAQ

Launch event FAQ

Do you have the stage and AV equipment for a launch show?

The 2F Convention Hall has a stage, LED main screen, sound and lighting, ready for reveal videos and presentations. For special effects or extra LED we connect you with our partners.

Is it suitable for media and influencer events?

With a glass-walled river-view hall, an illuminated facade at night and the 3F rooftop photo zone, the venue photographs and films beautifully. Photo walls and media walls can be installed.

Is there space for networking after the event?

Continue with a cocktail after-party in the 3F rooftop garden — a standing reception against the Han River night view.

Can we set up exhibition booths or product displays?

Product displays and booths can be installed in the hall and foyer; power and traffic flow are checked together in advance.
