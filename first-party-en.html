FAQ

First birthday FAQ

What is the minimum size for a first birthday party?

From 50 guests. A 50-guest party is held as a family celebration in a smaller room; 100–200-guest parties are set up in the 2F Convention Hall to match your numbers.

Are the dol table and photo table included?

The dol table and photo table setup package is ₩1,500,000 and includes banner installation. A professional MC for the doljabi and programme can be added for ₩200,000.

How is the meal served?

Choose a seated course or a buffet. Meals are free for children under 5, high chairs are provided, and menus can be adjusted for gatherings with many senior guests.

May we bring return gifts or a cake?

Return gifts are welcome and we set up a dedicated table. For cakes, rice cakes and other food items, let us know in advance and we will guide you.

Is parking and stroller access convenient?

Guests use the Banpo Han River Park lot (approx. 200 cars), free for 3 hours. Strollers travel by elevator, and the riverside promenade right outside is ideal for family photos after the meal.

How early should we book a weekend party?

We recommend booking weekend lunch slots 2–3 months ahead. Weekdays and evenings have more availability.
